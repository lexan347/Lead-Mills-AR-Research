# Site data and anchor policy

## Site

The study concerns the former Forest River Lead Works/Lead Mills landscape at the Salem–Marblehead boundary. Public descriptions and general visitor information may be cited in documentation. This repository does not yet assert a surveyed mill footprint or reconstruction origin.

## Coordinate requirements

Each production anchor must record:

- stable identifier and human-readable label;
- latitude and longitude in WGS84;
- altitude value and altitude reference;
- heading convention and value;
- coordinate acquisition method;
- estimated horizontal, vertical, and heading uncertainty;
- survey date and responsible party;
- associated asset version and checksum;
- publication status.

## Altitude policy

Do not label an altitude simply “meters.” Record whether it is:

- WGS84 ellipsoid height;
- orthometric elevation relative to a named geoid/datum;
- terrain-relative height;
- rooftop-relative height; or
- local-site-frame height.

## Public repository rules

- Example catalogs use null coordinates.
- Raw VPS scans and camera imagery require review before publication.
- Sensitive, restricted, or personally identifying material remains outside the public repository.
- Public anchors must not encourage visitors to leave authorized trails or enter unsafe areas.
- The site owner’s requirements supersede this repository’s technical plan.

## Field control

A surveyed marker or approved interpretive feature is preferred for validating position and heading. Natural features such as vegetation, shoreline, snow, and water are not treated as permanent control.
