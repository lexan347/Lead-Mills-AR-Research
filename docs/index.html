Research purpose

01

Reconstruct

Prepare a historically defensible, meter-scale mobile model with source provenance and uncertainty clearly recorded.

Localize

Compare GPS, visual positioning, and surveyed-reference methods instead of assuming a phone coordinate is precise.

Measure

Quantify position error, heading error, drift, localization time, repeatability, and recovery behavior.
