## Historical shoreline and wall — current user interpretation
User indicates the original river reached farther against a mapped retaining rock wall, later land filled/reclaimed. Their KML line is a **user triangulation interpretation**, not an instrument-surveyed masonry thickness or confirmed construction section.

Possible subsurface realizations: single stone retaining wall; stone-faced fill platform; multiple phases of retaining/reclamation. Model now as **line-aligned vertical surface only**, with thickness left UNKNOWN and explicit render-only placeholder if engine requires thickness. No model should imply that fill mass was all masonry.

## Dimensional confidence policy
User reports repeated historical map re-scaling/triangulation and self-assesses placement/size confidence **>90%**. Preserve that as `researcher_confidence_claim: >90%`, not independently quantified geodetic/measurement accuracy. Store revision history and original map geometry. Avoid converting subjective confidence into ±10% dimension error or survey-grade accuracy.
