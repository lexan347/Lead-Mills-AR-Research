## Interpretations — do not treat as fact
- **Working hypothesis:** tall smoking stack may exhaust a high-temperature furnace/lead-smelting operation, a boiler or steam-power plant; a 'forge' is one possibility but **not confirmed** and may not be the correct industrial term for a sheet/white-lead works.
- **Alternative:** stack could vent boiler exhaust from coal-fired steam machinery serving a different department. Black smoke alone cannot distinguish melting furnace, boiler, or forge.
- Emission locations depicted in 1848 artistic lithograph are historically suggestive, not measured source locations. Confirm with facility plans, descriptions of equipment, insurance maps, and archaeological evidence.
